About

I am a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Bo Zhao.

My research interests center on egocentric vision, human motion, and hand-object interaction, with a broader interest in real-to-sim data acquisition methods for embodied intelligence.

Discussions and collaborations are always welcome; feel free to reach out.
